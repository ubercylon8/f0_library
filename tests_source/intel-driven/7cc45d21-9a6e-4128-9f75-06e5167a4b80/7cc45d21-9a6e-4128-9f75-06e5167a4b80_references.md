The primary source documents The Gentlemen's post-compromise playbook: detailed
reconnaissance of endpoint protection before tailoring evasion (SecurityCenter2/service
enumeration), abuse of legitimate tools (Velociraptor repurposed as C2, PowerRun, Nmap,
AnyDesk), the encoded-PowerShell PDC-emulator probe, WMI/PsExec-based lateral movement,
Group Policy abuse for domain-wide payload deployment (GPME against an LDAP-addressed
policy object; final deployment via the NETLOGON share with a `--password` launcher),
and persistence through masquerading. These behaviors anchor stages 1–5.

## Provenance Notes

- **Stage 1 (T1518.001/T1087/T1082)** — reconnaissance-before-evasion posture and the
  encoded-PowerShell PDC probe: primary source (Trend Micro). The 1.bat-shape mass
  `net user`/`net group` enumeration (including `__vmware__*` localgroups and
  high-value account probes): ref 1 (Sophos CTU incident telemetry). EDR service-name
  probe list: refs 1–2.
- **Stage 2 (T1036/T1219)** — Velociraptor repurposed as the C2 channel: primary source
  (Trend Micro) and ref 1 (Sophos CTU). PerfLogs as the actor's staging directory
  ("not commonly scrutinized by security controls"): ref 1. Binaries/tasks named after
  Windows components / legitimate tools: ref 2 (Huntress). `client.config.yaml`,
  `client.writeback`, `/control` beacon path, client nonce and
  `Velociraptor <ver> (windows amd64)` User-Agent shapes: ref 6 (Velociraptor project).
  Safety deviation: the staged client is an inert text marker and the frontend is
  loopback-only — no real remote-access capability is deployed.
- **Stage 3 (T1047/T1021.002/T1021.001)** — WMI/PsExec lateral movement: primary source;
  rapid RDP pivots and `fDenyTSConnections` enablement: ref 1 (we query the value
  read-only). NetExec `nxc smb` argument shape: refs 1 and 5 (spec). The loopback
  `ADMIN$` + `ADMIN$\Temp` marker-transfer pattern follows the framework's LockBit 3.0
  stage-3 precedent (test `298cc137-ea39-4cf0-9b2d-b1c59a3cbacb`).
- **Stage 4 (T1484.001)** — GPME launched against an LDAP-addressed policy object and
  domain-wide deployment via `\\<domain>\NETLOGON\<payload>.exe --password <key>`:
  primary source (Trend Micro), corroborated by ref 1. Safety deviation: the policy
  object is a sandbox `{GUID}` tree inside ARTIFACT_DIR with authentic `gpt.ini`/PReg
  `Registry.pol` content; real SYSVOL, LDAP and Group Policy are never touched.
- **Stage 5 (T1053.005/T1543.003/T1136.001)** — the masqueraded task name
  `WindowsConnSvc` with `/sc minute /mo 2 /ru SYSTEM`: ref 2 (Huntress). Service
  installation for a covert channel (Cloudflared) and the privileged-account shapes
  (`net ... localgroup administrators <acct> /add`): ref 1 (Sophos CTU). Safety
  deviation: all three artifacts use test-owned names/actions with verified
  create-verify-delete cleanup.
- **Scope boundaries** — BYOVD via ThrottleBlood.sys/All.exe (ref 3) is deliberately
  out of scope: the Qilin test owns the BYOVD lane. T1486 encryption impact is owned by
  the LockBit 3.0 test. Threat-actor prominence context (GRIT Q3 2026: The Gentlemen
  12.9% vs Qilin 12.6% of observed ransomware mentions): ref 4.
